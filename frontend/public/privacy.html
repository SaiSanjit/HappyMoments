Privacy Policy for Happy Moments

Effective Date: October 11, 2026 | Last Updated: October 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Happy Moments ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website https://happymomentsindia.com and the Happy Moments mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard user information when you use our event planning, vendor directory, and mobile booking services.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, location, and login details when registering as a customer or vendor.
  • Vendor Content: Business name, services, event spaces, portfolio photos, pricing, and descriptions.
  • Inquiries & Quotations: Event dates, guest count, and service requests.

B. Device & App Permissions

  • Camera & Gallery: Used only when taking or uploading images for vendor portfolios, catalogs, or event spaces.
  • Microphone / Audio: Used strictly for speech-to-text input in our voice search feature. Audio is not retained or sold.
  • Internet: Required to access vendor listings and process requests.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Connecting customers with suitable event vendors (decorators, photographers, venues).
  • Transmitting booking inquiries and notifications.
  • Customer support and platform security.
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring system integrity.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information:

  • With event vendors you voluntarily contact for quotes or bookings.
  • With secure cloud service infrastructure providers (such as Supabase, Google Cloud).
  • When required by legal process or regulatory bodies.

5. Security & Retention

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) to protect data transmitted between your device and our servers. We retain information as long as your account remains active or as needed for business and legal purposes.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can access, correct, or request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. For dedicated instructions and submitting a deletion request, please visit our Account Deletion Page or email us directly at happymomentsforindia@gmail.com. Requests are handled promptly within 30 days.
