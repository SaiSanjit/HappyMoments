At Happy Moments, we respect your privacy and provide you full control over your personal data. In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and privacy regulations, you can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time.

1. Steps to Request Account Deletion

Method A: Request via Email (Fast & Direct)

  1. Send an email to our dedicated data privacy team at happymomentsforindia@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: “Account and Data Deletion Request - Happy Moments”.
  3. Include your registered email address and mobile number used during registration.
  4. State clearly whether you are a Customer or a Vendor/Business user.
Submit Email Request Now

Method B: Request via In-App Support

You can also navigate to Profile > Contact Us inside the Happy Moments app or on our website at happymomentsindia.com/contact to submit an account removal inquiry.

2. Types of Data Deleted

Upon confirmation and verification of your deletion request, the following information will be permanently purged from our primary databases:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, password hash, and phone number.
  • Profile Information: Gender, profile picture/avatar, address/location, and preferences.
  • Customer Search Data: All saved search filters, preferred vendors, and event criteria.
  • Inquiries & Bookings: Direct vendor inquiry messages, quotation requests, and personal notes.
  • Vendor Data (for Vendors): Business portfolio, catalog images, package details, and contact profiles.

3. Data Retained & Retention Period

We do not retain personal information once deleted, except where strictly required for compliance:

  • Financial & Invoice Records: If you have completed paid bookings, vendor transactions, or generated GST invoices, certain billing and transaction records may be retained for statutory periods required by applicable taxation and accounting laws.
  • Security & Audit Logs: Anonymized server logs may be maintained for up to 90 days for fraud prevention and system security, after which they are automatically purged.

4. Deletion Timeline

Once your request is received, our support team will verify ownership within 48 to 72 hours. All eligible account data will be completely and irreversibly purged from our active systems within 30 calendar days.

⚠️ Important Notice

Account deletion is permanent. Once completed, you will lose access to your saved searches, event portfolios, and vendor inquiries. You can create a new account at any time in the future.
